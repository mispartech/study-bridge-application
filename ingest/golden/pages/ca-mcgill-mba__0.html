Program at a glance

Your MBA, your path

The McGill MBA offers the flexibility to create an experience aligned with your goals and interests. Customize your experience with opportunities including:

→ Internship
→ International exchange
→ Practicum
→ Consulting projects
→ Case competitions

360 degree learning

We use multiple teaching methods with a focus on experiential learning and acquisition of new skills on emerging topics:

→ Artificial intelligence
→ Business analytics
→ Fintech
→ Sustainability
→ Design thinking
→ Innovation strategy
→ Ethical leadership

 

Tailored to your career goals

With personalized career support, McGill MBA graduates are positioned for career growth. From the moment you are admitted, you work one-on-one with a dedicated career coach to identify your strengths, refine your goals and chart a clear career path.

Career coaching
Personalized one-on-one guidance tailored to your chosen stream and goals.

Mentoring program
Mentorship from experienced business leaders.

Professional development
Leadership, career readiness and cultural adaptation workshops.

Interview preparation
Industry-specific coaching and interview preparation.

Employer engagement
Recruitment events and networking opportunities.

French language advantage
Access to complimentary French language lessons, expanding professional opportunities.

 

An alumni network without borders

Redefining your MBA for Tomorrow's Leaders

In a rapidly changing business environment, traditional education methods simply won't cut it. The McGill MBA program is anything but conventional; it’s designed for students with diverse backgrounds, skillsets, and future aspirations. We recognize that employers seek essential skills, like AI, fintech, data analysis, and design thinking. Our flexible specializations and 360 learning empower students to tailor their education, ensuring they gain the competitive edge needed to navigate and lead in the evolving business landscape.

Demetrios Vakratsas
Academic Director
